Warm-up · EBC · C1

Sponsorship deals

Concerts, festivals, clubs and creators often depend on sponsors' money. Pick a docket with your neighbour and talk about what sponsors really get, what fans put up with, and where you would draw the line.

Pick a docket with your neighbours and talk through its four questions. Explain your reasons, and ask what someone who disagrees would say.

All eight dockets

Docket 1

  1. Which brand do you connect with a festival, team or event you love? Does that make you like the brand more, or the event less?
  2. Is an artist 'selling out' when they accept a big sponsorship deal, or just being realistic about money? What would their fans say?
  3. Your student society needs €5,000 for its summer party. A fast-food chain offers it all, with its logo everywhere. What would you do, and on what conditions?
  4. Is there a real difference between an energy-drink brand sponsoring a tour and an oil company sponsoring one? Where would you draw the line?

Docket 2

  1. Berlin's Mercedes-Benz Arena became the Uber Arena in 2024. Did the new name change how you, or people you know, see the venue?
  2. Should a musician accept money from a company whose products they would never use themselves? How would fans react if they found out?
  3. Would you mind a festival full of brand stands if it made your ticket €40 cheaper? What would be too much?
  4. In 2024, after climate protests, Edinburgh's book festival and its investment-firm sponsor ended their deal. How hard should campaigners push events to drop sponsors if the events then struggle?

Docket 3

  1. Have you ever bought something because a team, player or musician you like was linked to it? What made the link convincing?
  2. Should artists and athletes have to say publicly how much a sponsor pays them? Who would benefit from knowing, and who would lose?
  3. You run a small football club. A betting company offers to pay for new kit for every youth team. What would you do, and why?
  4. From the 2026/27 season, Premier League clubs can't show betting companies on the front of their shirts. Fair protection for fans, or unfair on clubs that need the money?

Docket 4

  1. Think of an advert with a famous person that you found convincing, and one you didn't. What made the difference?
  2. Which matters more when choosing a sponsor: the money it brings, or what it says about you? Give an example.
  3. Your band is offered a sponsor whose values your fans dislike, but the money would pay for a whole tour. Would you take it, and how would you explain it?
  4. If a sponsor pays for a free concert in a poorer area, does the social benefit outweigh the advertising? Who really gains?

Docket 5

  1. Which club, band or event do you feel part of? How would you react if it suddenly signed a sponsor you disliked?
  2. Union Berlin's fans helped rebuild their own stadium. How much say should loyal fans have in which sponsors their club accepts?
  3. A company offers your university a new library if it can put its name on the building. Would you accept, and what limits would you set?
  4. Should companies that harm the environment be banned from sponsoring sport and culture, as tobacco companies are? Who would lose most?

Docket 6

  1. What sponsored content do you see most on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube? Can you always tell when it's an advert, and how?
  2. Which is worse for a creator: a badly made sponsored post, or a sponsored post for something they obviously don't use? Why?
  3. A brand offers you, a student creator, €2,000 for one post, but it must sound 'natural'. How would you handle it?
  4. Red Bull owns football clubs, racing teams and whole events. When does a sponsor become so powerful that the sport belongs to it?

Docket 7

  1. Have you ever been to a concert or match where the sponsor's presence annoyed you? What exactly did they do?
  2. Fans are loyal to artists, not brands. Who should benefit most from that loyalty: the artist, the sponsor or the fans themselves?
  3. You manage a young singer. A sponsor offers great money if she drops one song it dislikes from her show. What would you advise her, and why?
  4. Should state-owned companies, like national airlines, sponsor sport in other countries? Where does sponsorship end and politics begin?

Docket 8

  1. Which sponsor's logo do you remember from your childhood, on a football shirt, a TV show or a school event? Why has it stuck?
  2. Do you trust a product more when it sponsors something you care about? What would make you trust it less?
  3. Your student film festival can choose a bank that pays well or a local brewery that pays little but fits the mood. Which would you choose, and why?
  4. Could culture and sport survive without big sponsors? What would we lose, and what might we gain?
