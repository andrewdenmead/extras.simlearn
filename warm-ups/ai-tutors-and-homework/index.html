AI tutors and homework

AI tutors are getting better every year. What should that mean for school and for homework? Pick a docket, talk it through with your neighbour, and give reasons for your views.

Pick a docket with your neighbours and talk through its four questions. Explain your reasons, and ask what someone who disagrees would say.

All eight dockets

Docket 1

  1. What do you remember most about homework at school: what you learned, or how you avoided it?
  2. Does homework mainly teach the subject, or mainly teach discipline? Which matters more, and why?
  3. A strict teacher who never gives you the answer, or a patient tutor who explains everything: which helped you learn more?
  4. If AI can explain any topic perfectly, what are teachers for? What would a teacher who disagrees with you say?

Docket 2

  1. How do you use AI when you study: to explain, to check, to write, or not at all? Where do you draw the line?
  2. Why do some schools ban AI tools while others encourage them?
  3. Learning a language with an AI tutor that adapts to you, or in a class of real people who make mistakes too: which would you choose?
  4. What do children lose, and gain, when every question they have can be answered in seconds? What would a parent who disagrees say?

Docket 3

  1. Think of a subject you struggled with at school. What would have helped most: more time, a better explanation or more practice?
  2. Is the main purpose of school to pass exams, or to learn how to learn? Can it be both?
  3. Evenings free of homework, or homework that gets you better grades: if you were 15 again, which would you choose?
  4. Should school time go on practising skills, or on things only people can do together, like discussion and sport? Why?

Docket 4

  1. Who was the best teacher you ever had, and what made them good? Could an AI do what they did?
  2. Why do some people learn best alone, while others need a class around them?
  3. Revising with a friend who's slightly better than you, or alone with an AI tutor: which would you choose before an exam, and why?
  4. Should parents be able to see everything their child does on a learning app? What would a 15-year-old say?

Docket 5

  1. Have you ever had a tutor, a study group or a helpful older sibling? What difference did it make?
  2. Do you trust an AI's explanation as much as a teacher's? What makes you trust or doubt it?
  3. A teacher who returns your work in a week with personal comments, or an AI that marks it instantly in detail: which would you prefer?
  4. If AI makes lessons more efficient, should the time saved go on more teaching, shorter days or something else? Who should decide?

Docket 6

  1. What did you do instead of homework when you were supposed to be doing it?
  2. Finland is often praised for setting little homework. Would that work in Germany or the UK? Why?
  3. An AI tutor that is always patient, or a human tutor who sometimes loses patience but knows you: which would you learn more from?
  4. Should pupils learn how AI works before they're allowed to learn with it? What would a busy teacher say?

Docket 7

  1. Which subject did you need most help with at school, and who actually helped you?
  2. Will teachers' jobs get easier or harder with AI in the classroom? Why?
  3. Practising with an AI that quizzes you, or by explaining the topic to a friend: which makes it stick better, and why?
  4. How far should schools trust technology companies with children's learning? What would make you more comfortable?

Docket 8

  1. What's something you taught yourself, without a teacher or a course? How did you do it?
  2. Scotland, England and Germany all run their schools differently. What's one thing you'd copy from another country's schools?
  3. A class of 30 with one brilliant teacher, or a class of 30 each with an AI tutor and a teacher watching: which would you choose?
  4. In twenty years, will children learn more or less than we did? What would someone who disagrees with you predict?
