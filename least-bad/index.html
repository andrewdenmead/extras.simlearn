Ranking · Whole class or on your own

Least Bad to Worst

Twenty-nine things that happen in business. Put them in one line, from the least bad to the worst. You'll have to agree, or at least argue well.

Whole class

  • Draw a note. The class shouts where it goes: less bad or worse than the notes already in the line?
  • Tap a + gap in the line to put it there. Changed your minds? Tap a note in the line to pick it up again.
  • Argue it picks two neighbours in the line: someone has to say why one is worse.

On your own

  • Tap a note in the pile, then tap a + gap in the line below to put it there.
  • Tap a note in the line to pick it up and move it.
  • When you've finished, compare with a partner: which note did you put furthest apart?
