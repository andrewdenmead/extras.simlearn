All authors, studies, figures and countries here are fictional, written for class. Practise citing them as if they were real, but never cite them in real work.

Reporting verbs carry your judgement: shows and demonstrates say you trust the evidence; claims and asserts keep your distance. The builder suggests verbs from the judgements made in step 1.
