Where you stand

Your dots from the video. Tap Hold up to show one big on your screen, for the class line-up.

The line-up

  1. Your teacher picks a question. Everyone holds up their screen and stands along the room in order, from one end of the scale to the other.
  2. Talk to the person next to you: why are you standing here and not one step further along?
  3. Then find someone from the far end. Each of you explains your view in one minute. Before you reply, sum up their view in a sentence so that they agree it's fair.
  4. Anyone want to move? Say what changed your mind.

Being carefulIt seems to me that…From what the video showed, it looks as if…

Weighing it upOn balance, I'd say…The trade-off is that…
